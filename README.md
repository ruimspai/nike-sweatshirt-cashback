## Why the identical hoodie costs different amounts

Nike's own store rarely discounts current-season fleece. According to ShopBack's Singapore editorial team, Nike.com and Nike's physical stores price identically, and there's no channel discount for shopping online versus walking in. So if you buy a current-season Club Fleece hoodie from Nike directly, you pay S$99 or $80 and that's the end of the story — unless you're collecting on a layer underneath the price.

Where the real gaps show up:

**Past-season stock.** When Nike refreshes a fleece line, previous colourways clear out. JD Sports Singapore currently has the Nike Oversized French Terry Crew at S$99 marked down to S$39.60, a 60% cut. That's not a coupon; it's end-of-line inventory.

**Outlet stock.** ShopBack's Singapore guide puts Nike Factory Store outlet pricing at roughly 25–45% off past-season items, with the obvious tradeoff being limited sizes and colourways. No current-season models there.

**Nike Membership.** Free to join, and it unlocks member-only pricing and flash events worth up to around 20% on select items. That's a discount you can hold on top of everything else, because it's funded by Nike rather than by anyone else in the chain.

**Cashback.** This is the layer people forget, because it doesn't show up as a lower number on the product page. It shows up as money back in a wallet a few weeks later.

## When Nike sweatshirts actually get cheaper

Cashback is a constant; Nike's pricing is not. ShopBack's editorial team lays out the predictable windows:

In the US, Memorial Day weekend in late May usually brings site-wide percentage discounts on select styles, the early-August back-to-school window tends to run member exclusives on training apparel, and Black Friday through Cyber Week in late November is the deepest cut of the year. Separately, when a new fleece or shoe generation launches, the outgoing colourways often drop 25–40% within 60 to 90 days — that rolling clearance is more reliable than any single sale event.

In Singapore, the pattern leans on end-of-season clearance in roughly March to April and September to October, the 11.11 event, and Nike's Member Birthday Month in June. Different mechanism, same outcome: the fleece you want is cheapest when the season is turning over, not when you first thought about buying it.

The honest tradeoff: waiting saves money and costs you the size and colourway you actually wanted. Popular sizes in core colours are usually gone by the second week of a clearance window.
